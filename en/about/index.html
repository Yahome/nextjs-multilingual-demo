About us

Independent advisors for companies crossing borders

Founded in Shanghai in 2008, Meridian Partners now supports clients from three regional offices with a team of consultants, analysts and former industry executives.

Our story

Built where the work happens

Meridian began with a simple observation: companies expanding between Asia, Eurasia and the Gulf were receiving advice written far from the markets themselves. Strategies looked sound on paper but stalled at the first licence application or partner meeting.

So we built the firm the other way round. Our consultants live and work in the markets they advise on, speak the languages of their counterparts and have run operations themselves. That local depth is combined with one shared methodology, so clients get consistent quality wherever they expand.

Today we advise industrial groups, investors and fast-growing technology firms, and we remain independent: we accept no commissions from the partners, distributors or service providers we recommend.

Our principles

What clients can expect from us

Four commitments guide every engagement, from a two-week diagnostic to a multi-year programme.

  1. 01

    Independence

    Our only fee comes from our client. Partner and vendor recommendations are based on evidence, never on commissions.

  2. 02

    Local depth

    Native-speaking teams in each region bring direct access to regulators, industry bodies and decision-makers.

  3. 03

    Compliance first

    Sanctions, export-control and anti-corruption checks are built into every stage, not added at the end.

  4. 04

    Clarity

    We write for decision-makers: concise recommendations, clear trade-offs and no unnecessary jargon.
