Regional office · Shanghai

Mainland China

The world's largest manufacturing base and a demanding consumer market.

We help companies choose the right entry model, complete registration and certification, and find reliable partners — from the first market study to an entity of their own.

Key hubs
Shanghai, Shenzhen, Beijing, Chengdu
Conformity
CCC mark; NMPA registration for medical products
Working languages
Mandarin, English

Where we see opportunity

  • Advanced manufacturing and automation
  • Healthcare and life sciences
  • Premium consumer goods and cross-border e-commerce

What to plan for

  • Personal-information and data-export rules (PIPL)
  • ICP filing for websites hosted in the mainland
  • Contracts drafted for enforcement in Chinese courts or arbitration

Regional office · Moscow

Russia & EAEU

One conformity regime across five Eurasian markets.

We support certification, distribution and industrial partnerships across the Eurasian Economic Union, with compliance checks built into every step.

Key hubs
Moscow, St Petersburg, Kazan, Yekaterinburg
Conformity
EAC mark under EAEU technical regulations
Working languages
Russian, English

Where we see opportunity

  • Industrial equipment and components
  • Food, agriculture and packaging
  • Logistics corridors between Asia and Europe

What to plan for

  • Sanctions and export-control screening for every transaction
  • Payment and currency settlement routes
  • Russian-language labelling and documentation

Regional office · Dubai

GCC states

Six economies investing at speed in diversification.

From Dubai we cover all six Gulf Cooperation Council states, advising on licensing, regional headquarters, partners and government relations.

Member states
UAE, Bahrain, Saudi Arabia, Oman, Qatar, Kuwait
Conformity
G-Mark, SASO/SABER, ECAS
Working languages
Arabic, English

Where we see opportunity

  • Energy transition and industrial projects
  • Healthcare, education and giga-project supply chains
  • Regional headquarters and trading hubs

What to plan for

  • Agency and licensing rules that differ by state
  • Saudi regional-headquarters requirements for government contracts
  • Arabic-language contracts and documentation
